
## 2024-05-14 - [Soothing Assessment Experience]
**Learning:** Adding subtle animations (SVG waves, gentle characters) and clear progress indicators (StepIndicator) significantly reduces the perceived complexity of long assessments like BFI-10. Fluid transitions (entrance animations) make the experience feel more like a journey than a series of forms.
**Action:** Always include a visual progress indicator and a "calming" element (like WellnessBuddy) for mental health or sensitive assessments to improve user comfort and completion rates.

## 2024-05-15 - [ASMR Interactive Theme]
**Learning:** Global interactive effects (soft scales, glows) combined with a calming Wave background create a cohesive "soothing" brand identity. Compact, no-scroll layouts for assessments are essential for mobile usability, preventing fatigue.
**Action:** Use `asmr-hover` or similar gentle interactive classes project-wide to ensure a premium, responsive feel. Always verify "fold" visibility for assessment questions.
